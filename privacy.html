We do not store your raw IP address, browser user-agent, referrer, device identifiers, or location, and we set no cookies on the public site. We never show a destination's query string or fragment publicly.

What is public

A link's title, short link, a safe summary of the destination website, and its total click count are public and may appear in Latest Links and search. Do not put personal information in a title or destination you shorten.

Legal basis

We process data to provide the service you asked for (contract), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and free of abuse. We may also process data to comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare: hosting, DNS, the redirect service, and email routing.
  • Supabase: the database and API, hosted in Singapore.
  • A URL-safety screening provider, when enabled: receives destination URLs to check them for malware and phishing.

These providers may process data outside Thailand. We use them under their data-processing terms and share only what each service needs.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data; object to or restrict its processing; and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Email support@op4n.com. We acknowledge requests within 7 days and complete them within 30 days. If a public link contains your personal information, you can ask us to disable it. You may also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee.

Children

Op4n.com is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We will update the version and effective date above when this notice changes.
