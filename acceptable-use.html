Acceptable Use Policy

Version r1-2026-11-27 · Effective 27 November 2026

Op4n.com links are public. To keep them safe for everyone, you must not use Op4n.com to create, share, or point to any of the following.

Prohibited uses

  • Phishing: pages that steal credentials or payment details, or imitate a login.
  • Malware: malicious downloads, exploit pages, or unwanted software.
  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation: any sexual content involving minors. We report it to the relevant authorities.
  • Illegal content: content or activity that is clearly illegal.
  • Impersonation: pretending to be another person, brand, or organization, or misusing trademarks.
  • Harassment, hate, or scams: content that targets people with abuse or tries to defraud them.
  • Spam: unsolicited bulk promotion.
  • Abusing the service: evading rate limits, creating links in bulk with automation, hiding a destination to get around screening, or attempting to disrupt or access systems you are not allowed to.

What we do

We screen destinations, apply rate limits, and review reports. We may withhold a link from Latest Links and search, or disable it so it no longer redirects. Destinations or domains that are disabled repeatedly may be blocked from new links.

Report a link

Use Report link on any link card, or email support@op4n.com with the short link and what is wrong. This address is the abuse contact for Op4n.com and op4n.me. Serious reports (phishing, malware, child safety) are reviewed within 24 hours.
